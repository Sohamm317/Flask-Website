# Flask Blog guide

## Accounts
- Register at /register with a username (2-20 characters), an email, and a password entered twice. Usernames and emails must be unique.
- Log in at /login. "Remember Me" keeps you logged in after closing the browser.
- Forgot password: click "Forgot Password?" on the login page, enter your email, and a reset link is emailed. The link expires after 30 minutes.
- Logged-in users can't open the login or register pages.

## Account page (/account)
- Change your username, email, and profile picture (jpg or png only). Pictures are resized to 125x125.

## Posts
- Click "New Post" in the navbar (logged-in users only). A post needs a title and content.
- You can edit or delete only your own posts. Open the post and use the Update or Delete buttons. Delete asks for confirmation.
- The home page shows 5 posts per page, newest first, with page links at the bottom.
- Click an author's name to see all of their posts.

## Not available
- Comments, likes, search, and following other users do not exist yet.
- The About page is currently empty.
